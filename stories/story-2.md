# The Cartographer of Rain

Mara drew maps of storms no one else could predict. Each morning, she pinned blue thread across a blank page, following the pull she felt in her fingertips. By noon, clouds arrived exactly where the thread had crossed.

One dry summer, the village begged her to draw rain over their fields. Mara tried, but the thread sagged lifelessly in her hands. So she mapped something smaller: the path from every house to the old well.

That evening, the whole village met there with buckets, songs, and stories. Before dawn, the sky finally gathered to listen.
